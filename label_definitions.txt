advice: Tells the reader what to do: a practical suggestion or action, phrased as an instruction.
analysis: Explains how or why something happens, hedged ("can", "may", "often") or with a reason ("because", "so"), without telling anyone what to do or venting.
hot_take: States a bold, one-sided opinion as a flat claim ("should", "is not", "needs to stop"), with no hedging or reasoning.
complaint: Vents personal frustration about specific people or situations ("I'm tired of", "It's frustrating when", "Why do relatives…"), rather than making a general claim.
